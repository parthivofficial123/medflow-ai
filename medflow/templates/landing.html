One place for the paperwork around your care

  • AI bill analysis

    AI reads a PDF or photo of a bill, lists each charge and explains it in plain language. MedFlow, not the AI, does the math and flags anything it couldn't read.

  • Expense tracking

    Estimates, billed amounts and payments stay separate, so "not billed yet" is never mistaken for $0, and unpaid bills are easy to spot.

  • Appointment management

    Upcoming and past visits with follow-up tasks and reminders, each linked to the costs that came from it.

How it works

  1. 1

    Upload

    Choose a PDF, PNG or JPEG of a bill. It's read once in memory and never stored.

  2. 2

    Review

    Check every charge against the bill, fix anything misread, and untick what you don't want.

  3. 3

    Organize

    Approved charges become expenses you can mark paid and link to an appointment.

Privacy and limits, plainly

  • A student prototype built for a demo. It isn't a production service and hasn't been reviewed against HIPAA or any other healthcare standard.
  • Fictional bills only. Bills are sent to Google's Gemini free tier, whose inputs Google may review and use to improve its products.
  • General information, not advice. AI explanations can be wrong and are not medical, billing or insurance advice. Always check against the bill itself.
  • Your data is yours. You sign in with Google, and the database only returns rows that belong to your account.
